What's for Dinner?

Stop scrolling and just pick. Tap Spin and get one answer, in three modes.

OUT

A restaurant near you

Choose walking or driving distance, pick the kinds of food you're in the mood for, hide the ones you aren't, and skip chains or places you've been.

HOME

A recipe to cook tonight

A built-in library of 200+ original recipes. Scale servings, check off steps, run timers, and filter by pantry, budget and time.

DO

Something to do nearby

Parks, trails, museums and, when you want them, events.

Also in the app

  • Dietary and allergy filters
  • Pick together: 2 to 12 people vote
  • Shared dinners with invites and ratings
  • Weekly meal planner and shopping list
  • Directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze
  • Home screen widget on iPhone

Privacy first

No account, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold. Your location is used only while the app is open, and your history and favorites stay on your phone.
